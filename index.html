Torres Music Bingo

Hear it. Mark it. Shout Bingo.

Instead of numbers, we play songs you love and know. Every player gets a card full of song titles and artists. Hear a song on your card, mark it. Five in a row wins. Every round's champion dons the champion's chain until the next winner is declared.

1

Grab a card

Every game prints unique cards, so no two players have the same board. With playlists ranging from 30 to 80 songs, the possibilities are endless!

2

Listen up

The host spins short clips from a themed playlist. Name that tune, then find it on your card.

3

Claim the chain

First full line wins the round. The champion dons our champion's chain for a round, along with the bragging rights.

Pick a theme night

Choose from 45+ genres and themes, from Motown to Bad Bunny. Have something else in mind? We can build a custom playlist for your crowd.
