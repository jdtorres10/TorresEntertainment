Torres Music Bingo

Hear it. Mark it. Shout Bingo.

Instead of numbers, we play songs. Every player gets a card full of song titles and artists. Hear a song on your card, mark it. Five in a row wins, and the night's champion leaves wearing the gold chain.

1

Grab a card

Every game prints 50 unique cards, so no two players have the same board.

2

The host spins short clips from a themed playlist. Name that tune, then find it on your card.

3

Claim the chain

First full line wins the round. The night's champion takes home the medal and the bragging rights.
